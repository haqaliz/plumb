## Edge cases & error handling

- Negative margin (`0.87 ± -0.02`) and reversed intervals (`CI [0.93, 0.81]`) are
  refused at parse (Phase A) — never compared, never guessed.
- `margin = 0` degenerates to the D1 band (allowed).
- D1a applies to `PlusMinus` centre and `Approximate` value only; never to
  Interval/Range endpoints.
- Percent claims: band and margin/endpoints scale; the half-unit is scaled, never
  the scaled product's exponent (D7 trap).
- D13's nearest-boundary reach uses the artifact's own half-unit; mutation test
  mandatory.
- Do not edit `docs/planning/value-kinds-compare/prd.md` decisions; the
  binding-verdict D3 keeps its historical text with a supersession note.
